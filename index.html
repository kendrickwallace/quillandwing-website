How a letter travels

Three small ceremonies.

I

Write at the desk

Click your raven and the Writing desk opens. A fresh piece of parchment, a To, a Regarding, and room for what you mean to say.

II

Seal it and send a bird

Press the wax seal. Your bird takes the scroll, lifts off its perch and crosses your screen on its way out.

III

A reply comes home

When an answer arrives, your raven flies back and lands where it left. Open Deliveries to read the scroll.

A good listener. An excellent flyer.
A desk, not an inbox

Nothing here is in a hurry.

Deliveries

Every reply arrives as a scroll, kept beside the letter it answers.

Flight journal

A plain record of every flight: when it left, whether it landed, what came back.

Practice flight

Try the whole ceremony without an account. No real email leaves your Mac.

Your own mail

Connect Gmail or iCloud when you are ready, and your bird carries real letters.

A perch, not a window

Your courier waits on the desktop and lets your clicks pass through. Pause arrivals or hide the bird from the menu bar.

Kept on this Mac

Drafts and letters are saved on your Mac. Remove local data from Settings at any time.

Your inbox stays as you left it

Quill & Wing never removes or changes a single email in your primary account. Your feathered courier only carries letters; it does not tidy your mailbox.
